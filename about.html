MAITREYA MEDICAL MEDITATION MONASTERY

A Sacred Haven for Sakriya Vipassana, Healing & Prabuddha Bharat

Located at Chaparda, Taluka Kalamb, District Yavatmal (Maharashtra), Maitreya Medical Meditation Monastery was founded by Physician Zhan Bhikkhu Mitra Dr. Bodhi Ashok (MBBS, MD). Rooted in Shraman Samyak Prabuddha culture and Ambedkari Buddhism, our sanctuary blends traditional Vipassana, Mind-Body Balancing Sadhana ('MBBS'), and holistic medical relaxation to create a healthy, capable, prosperous, and enlightened society.

13 VISIONARY INSTITUTIONS

21st Century Nalanda University Vision

Under the auspices of Maitrey Bodh Sanstha, Zhanangbhoomi is actively developing 13 landmark institutions to serve humanity in health, education, culture, art, and spirituality:

1. Meditation Centre (Sakriya Vipassana & Vevibo Sadhana)
2. Ambedkari Buddhism - Nalanda University Campus
3. Maitreya Medical Diagnostics Centre (Doppler, CT, MRI)
4. Maitreya Ayurveda, Panchakarma & De-addiction Centre
5. Prabuddha Bharat Research Centre & Zen Library
6. Shraman Buddhas Gurukul (International School)
7. Acting, Drama, Art & Skill Institute for Youth
8. International 5-Star Hotel & Food Zone for Pilgrims
9. Maitreya Orphanage & Old Age Home
10. Samata Sainik Shaolin Martial Art Centre
11. Multi-Faith Pilgrim Dharamshala
12. Buddhas Art Gallery
13. Maitreya Printing Press & Publication
