21st Century Nalanda University Vision

1. Meditation Centre (Sakriya Vipassana & Vevibo)

Maitreya Medical Meditation Monastery | Chaparda

Dedicated silent Vipassana complex conducting 100-minute daily Sakriya Vipassana and Vevibo Sadhana under Dr. Bodhi Ashok and Yoga Teacher Bodhi Prakash.

2. Ambedkari Buddhism - Nalanda University Campus

Educational & Research Campus

A modern 21st-century Nalanda University dedicated to Shraman Samyak Prabuddha culture, Buddhist philosophy, literature, and social transformation.

3. Maitreya Medical Diagnostics Centre

Advanced Diagnostic Infrastructure

Equipped with Whole Body Color Doppler, Sonography, Echocardiography, X-Ray, CT Scan, MRI, and automated Pathology laboratories.

4. Maitreya Ayurveda, Panchakarma & De-addiction

Holistic Healthcare Sanctuary

Providing herbal medicine, Panchakarma detoxification, naturopathy, psychiatric counseling, and complete de-addiction therapy.

5. Prabuddha Bharat Research Centre & Zen Library

Bhikkhu Training & Knowledge Hub

Comprehensive archives of Buddhist manuscripts, Zen texts, Pali canon, and spiritual research with residential Bhikkhu training facilities.

6. Shraman Buddhas Gurukul

International School & College

Meditation-based international educational institution integrating mindfulness with modern science, ethics, and leadership.
