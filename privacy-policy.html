Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Static Website & Privacy Commitment

Zhanangbhoomi Buddhist Meditation & Vipassana Centre (https://zhanangbhoomi.org) is committed to protecting your privacy. This website is a static information platform designed to provide details about our monastery, Vipassana retreats, and medical meditation courses.

2. Information Collection

Because this is a static website, we do not directly collect, store, or process personal sensitive information on our web servers. Any contact forms on this website utilize standard email protocols (mailto:) or external communications (such as WhatsApp API) initiated explicitly by the user.

3. Official Course Registration Portal

Course registration, participant seat reservation, and attendee administration are managed through our official registration portal located at https://course.zhanangbhoomi.org. When you click "Register Now" or "Course Registration", you are redirected to our portal, which operates under its own security and privacy protocols.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or aggressive third-party behavioral profiling. Standard web server access logs maintained by cPanel shared hosting may record IP addresses, browser user agents, and page access timestamps purely for server security and network diagnostic purposes.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@zhanangbhoomi.org.
