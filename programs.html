DAILY MONASTIC PRACTICE

100-Minute Sakriya Vipassana Schedule

Practiced daily at 6:00 AM under the guidance of Physician Zhan Bhikkhu Dr. Bodhi Ashok and Yoga Teacher Bodhi Prakash.

Step Practice Name Description & Method Duration
1 1. Vandana, Sankalpa & Breathwork Vandana to all Tathagatas, Tirthankaras, Prophets & Sambuddhas, followed by sacred vow and conscious pranayama. 10 Mins
2 2. Sitting & Lying Yogasana Conscious postures including Padmasana, Vajrasana, Matsyendrasana, Sarvangasana, Halasana, Chakrasana, and Dhanurasana. 10 Mins
3 3. Standing Yogasana Surya Namaskar, Tadasana, Dhruvasana, Trikonasana, Padahastasana, Garudasana, and Shirshasana with full awareness. 10 Mins
4 4. Sakriya Swasanupassana & Kayanupassana (Step 1) Standing on toes with eyes closed, raising both arms, taking deep breaths and exhaling as arms lower. 10 Mins
5 5. Sakriya Vedananupassana / Kayanupassana (Step 2) Total Body Vibration with awareness — shaking shoulders, hands, legs, and spine to release deep bodily tension. 10 Mins
6 6. Sakriya Chittanupassana & Mantranupassana (Step 3) Jumping on toes in pure void awareness while chanting the sound 'Hoo' to experience pure existential presence. 10 Mins
7 7. Dhammanupassana (Step 4) Standing in neutral witness consciousness, observing all internal and external phenomena as they truly are. 10 Mins
8 8. Sakriya Muditanupassana / Anandnupassana (Step 5) Celebration of Life with awareness — expressing joyful gratitude and radiant bliss from a tranquil heart. 10 Mins
9 9. Shavanupassana (Step 6) Restorative deep relaxation lying on the back in Shavasana, letting go of all mental and physical effort. 10 Mins
10 10. Akriya Silent Vipassana / Vevibo Sadhana (Step 7) Silent non-doer meditation sitting in Sukhasana / Padmasana, abiding in effortless non-dual awareness. 10 Mins
