WELCOME TO ZHANANGBHOOMI

A Peaceful Haven for Silence, Healing & Dhamma

Nestled in a pristine natural environment, Zhanangbhoomi offers a serene space for seekers, meditators, and individuals longing for mental clarity and spiritual awakening. Rooted in traditional Buddhist philosophy and compassionate mindfulness, our monastery provides authentic guidance in Vipassana, Medical Meditation, and Mind-Body Balancing Sadhana.

THE DHAMMA PATH

Why Experience Zhanangbhoomi?

Vipassana Meditation

Experience self-observation and deep mental purification through authentic silent retreats designed to cultivate wisdom and equanimity.

Peaceful Environment

Immerse yourself in clean air, lush monastery gardens, and quiet contemplation halls away from city distractions.

Mind Body Balancing

Integrated Medical Meditation and gentle yoga practices intended to support relaxation, emotional equilibrium, and holistic well-being.

Buddhist Teachings

Learn timeless principles of Metta (loving-kindness), Karuna (compassion), Sila (moral discipline), and Panna (wisdom).

Nature & Silence

Surround yourself with mountains, trees, and soothing natural silence that naturally stills the racing mind.

Personal Transformation

Develop inner resilience, emotional clarity, and a deeper connection to your higher spiritual potential.
